03 / Alibaba Year 2: benefits and limitations

Year 2 value comes from consistent, focused operation.

Overall assessment: The store generated 247,769 impressions, 8,992 page views, 4,155 visitors, 2,877 search clicks and 399 BO from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, including 22 Inquiries and 377 Messages. Year 2 should maintain response quality, improve products and strengthen opportunity conversion.
Benefits

1. Retain accumulated traffic and data assets

SB TECH has an active store with 247,769 search impressions and 8,992 page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. Continuity allows the company to keep using accumulated behaviour data, product history and established search positions instead of starting over.

2. Foundation to expand international buyer groups

Over 12 months the store generated 399 BO, including 22 Inquiries and 377 Messages; May–Sep 2026 alone recorded 248 BO, including 13 Inquiries and 235 Messages. Year 2 can focus on products suited to each market instead of publishing broadly.

3. Build trust with clear factory information

On Alibaba.com, SB TECH is presented as a manufacturer in Binh Duong with Hanger, Ice Box, Camping Ice Box, Lunch Box, Home Goods and Polybag product groups. In Year 2, the store should consistently present factory images, production capacity, quality control, sample products and delivery capability. This helps buyers assess the company before sending an Inquiry or requesting a quotation.

Limitations / risks

1. A maintenance fee does not create orders by itself

Store performance shows 22 Inquiries in 12 months. Traffic or Inquiry volume should not be used as a substitute for actual business performance.

2. Store quality is not yet consistent

The overall rating is 2 stars; 159 of 532 products have a Product Score of 5.5 or above, and current-month RFQ quotations are 0. Renewing without rebuilding priority products and RFQ/Inquiry handling may keep generating signals but few qualified opportunities.

3. Investment must be paired with operating discipline

Year 2 requires an owner for product uploads, Inquiry handling, quotations, samples, follow-up and order reconciliation. Without an internal owner, the package benefits will be limited.

Role of Alibaba.com: Alibaba.com is a channel for generating and screening B2B demand. Verified can support credibility and reach, but results still depend on suitable products, competitive quotations, fast responses and SB TECH’s follow-up capability.
04 / Proposed 12-month plan

Year 2 is divided into four phases, each with verifiable deliverables.

Phase 1 · After renewal

Fix current gaps

  • Review the entire store against current Product, Marketing and Service indicators.
  • Select 30–50 priority products to improve titles, images, specifications, MOQ and quotation content.
  • Add factory information, production capacity, quality control, sample products and delivery capability.
  • Assign an owner to respond to Inquiries and Messages and maintain stable response time.
  • Identify product groups below quality standards and build a first-quarter improvement priority list.
  • Plan to add 468 valid products, raising the total from 532 to 1,000 products, in line with the Product 5★ target.

Deliverable: an improvement list, priority product groups, a product upload plan and a clearly assigned response process.

Phase 2 · 27 Nov 2026–26 Jan 2027

Expand the catalog and improve the Product index

  • Launch the first product upload wave, prioritising groups directly linked to SB TECH production capability.
  • Add about 156 products, bringing the total valid product count to about 688.
  • Standardize images, titles, specifications, MOQ, applications and customization options for each product.
  • Focus keywords on buying intent: custom cooler box, camping ice box, PP hanger, lunch box, private label and OEM where relevant.
  • Segment buyers by market, use case and MOQ instead of using one reply for all.

Deliverable: an expanded catalog, new listings meeting content standards and monthly Product Score tracking.

Phase 3 · 27 Jan–26 Apr 2027

Continue publishing products and convert BO into quotations

  • Add about 156 products, bringing the total valid product count to about 844.
  • Check content quality across all new products and improve products below a Product Score of 5.5.
  • Classify BO by: right product, right MOQ, customization needed, not suitable and insufficient information.
  • Set up three quotation templates by product group, covering MOQ, lead time, packaging, logo/customization and sample terms.
  • Record stages: responded, quoted, sample sent, negotiating, lost and the reason.

Deliverable: about 844 valid products, improved listing quality and a clearly owned BO handling process.

Phase 4 · 27 Apr–26 Oct 2027

Complete the Product 5★ milestone and review Year 2

  • Add the remaining 156 products to reach the target of 1,000 valid products.
  • Review the entire catalog, remove duplicates or substandard content and continue improving Product Score.
  • Compare month-on-month changes in Product, Marketing and Service.
  • Assess BO, quotation, sample and order quality to identify product groups worth continued investment.

Deliverable: the 1,000 valid product target, progress toward Product 5★ and a Year 2 performance review.
